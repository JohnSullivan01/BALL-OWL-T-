Explore the facility

The building wasn't put up for one sport or one athlete. Two 55-foot cages, pitching and fielding machines, an open turf floor and gym equipment — enough room for baseball, softball, basketball, soccer, football and the strength and speed work behind all of them.

How booking works

01

Pick what you need

Private training, a cage, or team time. Each one shows its length, price and who or what you're booking before you go any further.

02

Choose a time

Open slots only, in Eastern time, at 116 East Neshannock Avenue. Cancellation terms are shown before you pay, not after.

03

Sign the waiver, show up

Confirmation arrives with the waiver and facility rules attached. Under 18 means a parent signs. Then just turn up and train.

Questions before you book

What age or level is this for?

[AGE RANGE AND LEVELS — the youngest age taken, the oldest, and whether adults are welcome. The site doesn't say today, and it's the first thing a parent checks.]

What should we bring to the first session?

[WHAT TO BRING — glove, bat, helmet, cleats or turf shoes, water. One short list from the owner so nobody arrives short.]

Do I have to sign anything?

Yes. Every person using the facility completes a waiver and acknowledges the use and rules forms. Anyone under 18 needs a parent signature. All three are linked in the footer and sent with your confirmation.

Can parents watch?

[OWNER'S ANSWER — where parents wait, whether there's seating, and any rules about being on the turf during a session.]

What's your cancellation policy?

[POLICY — notice required, whether sessions can be rescheduled, and what happens to a missed slot. Shown again at checkout.]

Can a team rent the whole floor?

Team, group and individual rentals are all available, and a visiting coach can run their own practice. Team time goes through a request so we can confirm the space before you pay.
